# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participation in this project should be respectful, constructive, and focused
on improving the software. Welcome people regardless of experience,
background, identity, or perspective. Give actionable feedback, assume good
faith, and respect privacy.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal
attacks, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private
information, filesystem paths, or disk contents without permission.

## Scope

This standard applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and any
other space used to represent the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior by opening a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/trsdn/SpaceMender/security/advisories/new)
if the report contains sensitive details, or by contacting the maintainer
directly otherwise. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate this
standard, and may block repeat offenders from the project.
